How we went from 10,363 → 1,123

1
Started with SEC full universe All US-listed companies from SEC EDGAR — 10,363 listings
2
Removed non-operating entities Filtered out banks, bancorps, financial institutions, trusts, funds, ETFs, REITs, holding companies, and realty firms — these don't generate operating free cash flow. −5,407 removed
3
Deduplicated OTC tickers Removed duplicate OTC listings of the same underlying company (e.g. foreign ADR shadows). −370 removed → 4,586 remaining
4
Removed micro-caps ≤ $50M market cap Too illiquid and high-risk for meaningful value investing signals. −256 removed
5
Removed tickers with no market cap data Yahoo Finance returned no market cap for these — data too sparse to be useful. −70 removed → 4,260 in universe
6
Removed companies with missing EV or FCF data Yahoo Finance returned no Enterprise Value or Free Cash Flow — cannot compute the ratio. −297 removed
7
Removed companies with negative FCF A negative FCF/EV ratio has no value-investing interpretation — these companies are burning cash. −2,840 removed
8
How FCF/EV is computed FCF is the average of up to 5 values: TTM (trailing twelve months) + last 4 annual years. Enterprise Value is the live value as of the Friday the data was pulled.
9
Final ranked universe Companies ranked by FCF/EV % — higher means more free cash generated relative to price paid. 1,123 companies
